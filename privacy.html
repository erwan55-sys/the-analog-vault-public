THE ANALOG VAULT

Privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026.

The Analog Vault is an independent, private application operated by the owner of The Analog Vault eBay selling account. It is not affiliated with eBay.

Purpose and current scope

The Analog Vault helps its owner manage listings for personal collectibles on eBay. It supports temporary seller authorization, eBay account deletion notifications, listing preparation and a private ChatGPT connection for the owner. Commercial publication, buyer messaging, offers and ratings are not enabled in the current release.

Information processed

During seller authorization, the service processes the eBay authorization code, access and refresh tokens, user ID and username. eBay seller tokens and identity fields stay in volatile memory until session reset, authorization expiry, restart or a matching deletion notification. The application stores a hash of the pending OAuth state and a connection result without the seller identity. The service verifies signed account deletion notifications and uses their identifiers to determine whether they concern the connected owner. It does not intentionally retain notification contents or collect buyer messages or order data.

Cloudflare D1 stores owner-created listing drafts, revisions and links to listing photos. D1 Time Travel can retain earlier versions of drafts for the period applicable to the Cloudflare plan. If the owner uses the mobile upload, the service prepares the image and removes embedded metadata before uploading to eBay Picture Services. The service keeps the eBay image reference and expiry. Do not upload photographs or enter draft text that contains personal information unrelated to the listing.

ChatGPT connection and service providers

The owner chooses whether to connect the private ChatGPT plugin. When the plugin is used, OpenAI processes the data returned by the requested tools, which may include the seller username, listing summaries, drafts and associated photo references. The owner's eBay credentials are never sent to ChatGPT. OpenAI handles plugin interactions and conversation data under its privacy policy and the owner's account settings. Removing access to the plugin does not delete prior chat history.

Cloudflare hosts the service and stores its application secrets. GitHub Pages hosts these public documents. eBay handles seller authorization, listing verification and image hosting. The Analog Vault does not sell personal information or use eBay user information for advertising.

Deletion and privacy requests

A verified notification with a matching seller identifier clears the volatile eBay seller session. The owner can also disconnect the plugin, clear the seller session and revoke access from the eBay account. The eBay service controls retention of images uploaded to eBay Picture Services. You can contact the operator through The Analog Vault seller account on eBay about privacy requests.

Changes

This policy applies to the private owner-only application and will be updated as data handling changes.
