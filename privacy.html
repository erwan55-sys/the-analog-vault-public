THE ANALOG VAULT

Privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026.

The Analog Vault is an independent, private application operated by the owner of The Analog Vault eBay selling account. It is not affiliated with eBay.

Purpose and current scope

The application is being developed to help its owner manage listings of personal collectibles. The application supports application-level authentication, eBay account deletion notifications and temporary owner-only seller OAuth. Listing management, messaging and external AI integrations are not yet enabled.

Information processed

The service uses application credentials to authenticate with eBay and temporarily processes signed account deletion notification contents, including account identifiers where supplied by eBay, solely to authenticate and respond to the notification. During owner authorization, the service temporarily processes the authorization code, access token, eBay user ID and username to verify the seller identity. Access tokens and identity fields are held only in volatile server memory until expiry, session reset, restart or a matching deletion notification. Refresh tokens are not retained. No seller authorization tokens or user profiles are persisted in the database. Buyer messages and order information are not collected. Notification contents are not intentionally logged, retained or exported by the application. Public signing keys may be cached temporarily.

Security and service providers

The service is hosted on Cloudflare Workers and communicates with eBay over HTTPS. These public information pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Application secrets are stored as encrypted Worker secrets. Cloudflare, GitHub and eBay may process technical connection information in providing their services, subject to their own policies and applicable law. We do not sell personal information or use eBay user information for advertising.

Deletion and privacy requests

The service authenticates eBay account deletion notifications and discards their contents after processing. The current architecture has no persistent eBay user records to erase. A verified matching account deletion notification clears the temporary seller session; an ambiguous notification with no comparable identifier also clears it as a precaution. The owner can clear the session manually and revoke application access at eBay. You may contact the application operator through The Analog Vault seller account on eBay concerning privacy requests. You may also manage your eBay account and application access through eBay. The service will require a documented deletion mechanism and an updated privacy policy before enabling persistent user data or external AI processing.

Changes

This policy will be updated before new data-processing features are activated. The current application is for the account owner only.
